Workspace application icon

Resources/Workspace.png is the cube from NextBSD.png in nextbsd-theme
(https://github.com/nextbsd/nextbsd-theme), without the logo's black
square: the black reached from the edges was made transparent, the
anti-aliased rim un-blended from black, and the cube centred on a square
canvas and scaled to 256 x 256 RGBA. nextbsd-theme is BSD 2-Clause,
Copyright (c) 2026 Joseph Maloney.

Hard disk icon

HardDisk.tiff is copied unchanged from GNUstep GWorkspace's FSNode framework
(Resources/Images/HardDisk.tiff), using the installed FSNode resource. It is
bundled locally so drive icons do not depend on GWorkspace being installed.
